12K–13K Followers

The Problem

Autoshop Expert offers vehicle nano coating and detailing, and cut and polish services. They are the only authorised partner in Sri Lanka of the international brand Tonyin, yet sales had dropped because their marketing performance was weak. Few people knew how good the service was.

Our Strategy

Before creating anything, we built a marketing proposal and a full content calendar tailored to the brand. We planned what to post, when to post it and why, so every piece of content had a job to do.

The Solution

We ran their social media and digital marketing end to end, following the plan: transformation-style videos that show the before and after of nano coating, detailing and polishing, supported by graphics and consistent posting.

The Result

Followers grew to 12K-13K, and orders went up. One single video brought in 200+ followers and passed 150,000+ organic views.

The Content
Beyond the numbers: the audience we built on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok turned into real customers, and orders went up.
