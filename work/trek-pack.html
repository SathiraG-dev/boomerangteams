Trek Pack

Making luggage look like an adventure.

100 → 12,000 Facebook Followers
500K+ Organic Views
20K+ Views On Top Reels
The Problem

Luggage is not exactly a scroll-stopping product. Trek Pack had a small audience and needed wider awareness before people would think of them first.

Our Strategy

We stopped selling bags and started selling the journey. Creative, product-focused videos put the luggage range into real travel moments.

The Solution

Creative product videos that showcase the range, reels built for Instagram and Facebook reach, and content that connects the brand with travellers.

The Result

About 100 to 12,000+ followers, 500,000+ organic views, and several Instagram reels passing 20,000 views.

Beyond the numbers: the audience we built on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok turned into real customers, and orders went up.
