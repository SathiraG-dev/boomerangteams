Milton Motors

From 86 followers to 18K, and 5 million+ organic views.

86 → 18,000 Facebook Followers
5M+ Organic Video Views
150K Views On Top Video
The Problem

A strong product with almost no digital presence. Milton Motors had 86 followers, and the few people who noticed them were not turning into enquiries or orders.

Our Strategy

We studied how leading automotive brands hold attention online and found the gap: nobody in this space was making vehicles entertaining. We built a content engine around short, punchy videos and bold graphics that were recognisable at first glance.

The Solution

Video-first content built for organic reach, a redesigned graphic identity, and consistent posting backed by Meta Ads to turn attention into customers.

The Result

86 to 18,000+ followers and more than 5,000,000 organic views. Individual videos reached 150K, 80K and 70K views.

The Content
Beyond the numbers: the audience we built on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok turned into real customers, and orders went up.
